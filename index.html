StreamDiffusionV2: An Open-Sourced Interactive Diffusion Pipeline for Streaming Applications

Code

We are an open-source interactive diffusion project for real-time streaming applications — scalable across diverse GPU setups, supporting flexible denoising steps, and delivering high FPS for creators and platforms.

Abstract

Generative models are reshaping the live-streaming industry by redefining how content is created, styled, and delivered. Our first release, StreamDiffusion, powered fast and creative image-based live-streaming products (e.g., Daydream, TouchDesigner) but hit limits on temporal consistency. Recent video diffusion models (e.g., CausVid) offer smoothness and efficiency in offline video generation, yet it lack designs tailored to online live-streaming. StreamDiffusionV2 closes that gap: a fully open-source, streaming-living pipeline for online interactive streams with video diffusion models. It fuses StreamVAE, sinked rolling-KV, dynamic pipeline parallelism, and a motion- aware noise controller to stay stable under high-speed motion. To cater to users at every level, StreamDiffusionV2 scales seamlessly across diverse GPU environments and supports flexible denoising steps (e.g., 1-4 steps). Without TensorRT and quantization, StreamDiffusionV2 reaches 42 FPS on 4× H100 with a 4-step model, 17.6 (resp. 8.9) FPS on 2× (resp. single) RTX 4090 with a 1-step model—making state-of-the-art generative live streaming practical and accessible from individual creators to enterprise platforms.
